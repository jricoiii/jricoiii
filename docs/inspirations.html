Artists and color

  • Stanley Whitney

    Loose grids, adjacent colors, and the freedom to improvise within a recurring form.

    Source

  • Mary Heilmann

    Geometry with an intuitive use of color. Look at how an edge, a gap, or an unexpected color changes the balance of a painting.

    Source

  • Jennifer E. Padilla

    Symmetry, calligraphy, and the craft of making mathematical forms.

    Source

  • Emma Kunz

    Geometric drawings that make repeated lines, layered symmetry, and color feel alive.

    Source

  • Wang Ge (Pearl)

    Colored sand and raised wire boundaries. Notice what a physical edge adds to a field of color.

    Source

  • Nicole Pietrantoni

    Accordion folds and repeated modules that turn printed landscapes into sculpture.

    Source

  • Joomchi and Jiyoung Chung

    Korean paper manipulation as a way to think about layered color, fiber, and a surface that can behave like fabric.

    Source

  • Merete Rasmussen

    Continuous sculptural forms. Look at the open space as carefully as the colored surface.

    Source

Tilings, puzzles, and play

  • Nat Banting · Upscale Pattern Blocks

    A familiar shape set becomes a new set of problems when the scale changes.

    Source

  • MMACA · Mathematics Museum of Catalonia

    Hands-on mathematical exhibits, including puzzles about which colors can sit next to each other.

    Source

  • Donald Bell · Loyd Polyominoes

    A compact set of polyominoes with enough variety for several challenges.

    Source

  • Pentoma · Tetracairo

    Four Cairo pentagons become joined pieces with possibilities in two and three dimensions.

    Source

  • János Saxon Szász · Poly-Universe

    A shape-and-color system that invites both open composition and optional challenges.

    Source

  • Peter F. Esser · Polyforms

    An extensive collection of related puzzle families. Look at how one shape rule turns into many physical pieces.

    Source

  • Katamino Family

    Colorful polyominoes and a range of packing challenges. What makes a small set inviting to start with?

    Source

  • Mike McManaway · Tantrix

    Color paths create local matching rules and progressively harder loop puzzles.

    Source

  • Kadon · Edge-matching puzzles

    A collection of wood and acrylic puzzles built around the consequences of matching one edge at a time.

    Source

  • STICCY · STACCS

    Projected forms and geometric illusions that make a card game look dimensional.

    Source

  • Flatout Games

    A reference for turning spatial challenges into games people can quickly sit down and play.

    Source

  • Polyflaptiles · The Poly Pages

    A bisected hexagon provides a starting unit for a family of related shapes and ornamental tilings.

    Source

  • POLYFORMATION · Two Patterns, One Solid

    A 3D puzzle form with a square reading on one face and a triangular reading on the other. Maker attribution remains open.

  • POLYFORMATION · Four Shapes, Two Worlds

    A study of families of pieces arising from the square-to-triangle dissection. Maker attribution remains open.

    Related research on the dissection

  • Robert Fathauer · Fractal tilings

    The same geometric motifs can read differently at several scales. An invitation to explore scale and color in my own Cairo pieces.

    Source

  • Matsys · Confluence Park

    A reference for carrying a repeating geometric idea into architectural form.

    Source

  • Lovevery · Wooden puzzles

    A reminder that thickness, rounded edges, weight, and finish help determine whether pieces invite handling.

    Source

  • HABA · Creative Stones

    Wooden blocks that can read as flat patterns or become small sculptural arrangements.

    Source

  • Seven2 Wall Art and Gabriel Schama

    Layered laser-cut wood, nested openings, shadows, and color. Look at what changes as the eye moves between layers.

  • Jewel · Heart of the Ocean

    Ocean data help shape an installation’s changing light and sound.

  • Jewel · Seven Sisters

    Astronomical observations inform the light and sound of seven glass forms.
