Backgammon

I played my first game in 2018, after my wife and I bought a Crisloid board. I liked it right away. The dice give you something to work with, and the choices you make with a roll can still surprise you. I like going over a position with another player afterward and finding out what they were paying attention to when they chose a different move.

Backgammon at Ohlone

I started a backgammon club at Ohlone College. I wanted there to be a table where students could sit down, learn the game, and get to know somebody over a board. If you’ve never played, I’d be happy to show you how. Email me about playing at Ohlone.

I play online as jricoiii on Backgammon Galaxy. You can also read my older Games post, including the story of that first board.
