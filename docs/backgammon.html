Backgammon

I played my first game in 2018, after my wife and I bought a Crisloid board. I liked it right away. The dice give you something to work with, and the choices you make with a roll can still surprise you. I like going over a position with another player afterward and finding out what they were paying attention to when they chose a different move.

Backgammon at Ohlone

I started a backgammon club at Ohlone College. I wanted there to be a table where students could sit down, learn the game, and get to know somebody over a board. If you’ve never played, I’d be happy to show you how. Email me about playing at Ohlone.

Smiles & Dice Club FAQ

What is Smiles & Dice?

We’re an Ohlone College student club that gets together to play games. We started with backgammon and still bring out the boards. Come with friends or on your own, join a game at the table, or bring a game you’d like to share.

Why did the club start?

I wanted more people to discover backgammon and have a place to take a break from class and meet someone new. As the club grew, we opened the table to other games. I’d also love to help players who want to study backgammon in more depth and eventually bring students from different Bay Area colleges together for a tournament.

When and where do you meet?

Email me for the current meeting time and room. I’m the club’s faculty advisor. A club member or I can also send you a Discord invite. You can find us on Instagram.

Can I come if I’ve never played backgammon?

Yes. Sit down and play a game with us. If you’d like to get a feel for it first, try my introductory slides or the two videos from Backgammon Galaxy below. Ask at a meeting about borrowing a board or book.

Where can I play or study online?

Try Backgammon Galaxy to play other people or Extreme Gammon to analyze positions and get feedback on your play.
