My Art

Browse my Inspiration Catalog

I want to make mathematical objects that people want to touch, move, arrange, and return to. Someone might turn a piece in their hand, try it next to another, and see a relationship they want to follow. I’m interested in what happens when people have enough structure to get started and enough freedom to make something of their own.

Sometimes I start by making a composition I like. Then I wonder what pieces or relationships could make it possible, and what else someone could make with those same pieces. Other times I start with a few shapes and see what they let me do. I like being able to leave an arrangement on the table for a while and come back to take it apart or change one part of it.

What I’m exploring

  • Diagonal and curved tiles. I’m trying out small sets where turning a piece can change a path or pattern across the whole arrangement.
  • Wood, fit, and color. I’ve been laser cutting sets of polygons and trying different sizes and markings. I’m curious how the feel and fit of a piece affect what someone chooses to do with it.
  • From flat pieces to layered forms. I’d like to see how pieces that fit together on a table could give rise to something with height.
