Professor of Mathematics

I teach Statistics and Math for Liberal Arts. In both classes, we start with something you can explore. You’ll notice patterns, compare ideas with classmates, and explain what you think is happening.

Courses

STAT C1000

Introduction to Statistics

We use real data to ask questions about the world and decide what the evidence lets us say.

  • Explore data and compare groups.
  • Use simulations and confidence intervals to think about chance and uncertainty.
  • Investigate a question in your own words and explain what you found.

Math for Liberal Arts

Patterns, games, and design

We make things, play games, and look for the mathematical ideas that emerge.

  • Arrange tiles, count possibilities, and look for patterns.
  • Explore symmetry, repeats, and what happens when you change a rule.
  • Use tilings and geometric constructions to develop an original design.

About me

I studied mechanical engineering and secondary education at the University of Michigan. I’ve taught at Ohlone since 2016. One of my favorite classroom moments is when students point out something I didn’t see and we stop to make sense of it together.

Outside class, I like walking and biking near Quarry Lakes and Alameda Creek, playing backgammon, and listening to jazz and reggae. I’m also exploring how to make mathematical objects people can pick up, move, and arrange.
